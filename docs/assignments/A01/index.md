# Portfolio Analysts

Portfolio #1 [Ethan Liu](BiggestWaffler.github.io)
  This portfolio is an exceptional example on ease of locating specific projects. Not only is there a hotlink permanently at the top of the page that brings someone, such as an employer, looking through this portfolio directly to Liu's project's page, each project block comes with its name, a short description of what it is, and what was used to make the project, allowing someone with even only a fragmentary memory of what the project was to be able to find it easily and quickly. Additionally the entire page is concise with all employer important information, such as resume and contact information, easily available with no unnecessarily large blocks of text or other distracting factors.
    While being exceptionally concise, that conciseness comes at the cost of complexity. Only one project has their full source code uploaded to GitHub, thus only allowing a colleague to reproduce that single project easily without having to ask many questions. A sufficiently advanced colleague however may be able to reproduce some of the other not-on-GitHub projects due to the fact the project descriptions contain some technical jargon on how the product was built, allowing said advanced colleague to be able to make educated guesses on how the project was built. However, unless you are a professional programmer with years of experience, the project descriptions given in this portfolio are not sufficient to reproduce the products listed without asking many questions.
    Similarly, this portfolio shows merely the end results. Within the experience section, Liu gives only bullet points on what his roles were during his internships and jobs, with no timeline on how and why certain decisions were made for the projects worked on in those jobs. The same applies to the projects page, where the descriptions and even inside the interactive projects only show what the project ended up becoming, and not how the projects were developed and why certain methods were chosen.
    Lastly however, this portfolio is a gold standard when it comes to what an employer would be looking for. All relevant information is written in a highly professional, concise manner, while using professional level terminology that shows that Liu has experience in his subject matter.
